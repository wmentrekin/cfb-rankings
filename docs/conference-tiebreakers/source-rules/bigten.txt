BIG TEN CONFERENCE — 2024 FOOTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP GAME TIEBREAKER
(Announced Aug 26, 2024; first season matching No. 1 and No. 2 in the overall standings)
Supplied by repo owner 2026-09-11.

The Big Ten championship is decided by a game between the two teams with the best winning
percentage in conference games following the regular season.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION A -- TWO TEAMS TIED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. If two teams are tied for the No. 1 position, both participate and the winner of the game
   between them is the first-place team. If they did not play, move to step A.3.
2. If two teams are tied for the No. 2 position, the winner of the game between them is the
   representative. If they did not play, move to the next step.
3. Record against all common conference opponents.
4. Record against common opponents with the best conference record, proceeding through the
   common conference opponents based on their order of finish within the conference standings.
5. Best cumulative conference winning percentage of all conference opponents.
   (a) In the event of an unbalanced schedule (fewer than nine conference games played), compare
       on the best cumulative conference winning percentage of all conference opponents
       REGARDLESS of how many conference opponents each team played. If winning percentage is
       equal for all conference opponents, move to the next step.
6. Highest ranking by SportSource Analytics (team Rating Score metric) following the regular season.
7. Random draw among the tied teams conducted by the Commissioner or designee.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION B -- THREE OR MORE TEAMS TIED FOR FIRST
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Peel-off and restart semantics (verbatim intent):
- After head-to-head, if a step produces a clear No. 1 by itself, that team is selected and the
  remaining teams REVERT TO THE BEGINNING of the applicable procedures (three tied -> the other
  two go to the first step of the two-team procedures; four tied -> the other three go to the
  first step of the three-team procedures).
- If a step produces two teams tied for No. 1, both are selected; they progress through the
  two-team procedures to decide ranking.
- If a step produces two teams tied for a position OTHER than No. 1, the winner of the game
  between them is the representative; if they did not play, move to the two-team procedures.
- If three or more remain tied after any step, move to the next step with the remaining teams.
- If a step eliminates a team, that team SHALL NOT be pulled back into the tiebreaker for any
  future step.
- If all teams involved are still tied after any step, all continue to the next step.

1. Winning percentage in games among the tied teams:
   (a) If all teams involved did not play each other, but one team defeated all other teams in
       the tie, that team is removed from the tiebreaker and the remaining teams revert to the
       beginning of the applicable process.
   (b) If all teams involved did not play each other and no team defeated all others, move to
       the next step.
2. Winning percentage against all common conference opponents played by all other teams
   involved in the tie.
3. Winning percentage against the next highest placed common opponent in the standings in
   order of finish.
4. Best cumulative conference winning percentage of all conference opponents.
   (a) Same unbalanced-schedule rule as Section A.5(a).
5. Highest ranking by SportSource Analytics (team Rating Score metric) following the regular season.
6. Random draw among the tied teams conducted by the Commissioner or designee.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSTSEASON INELIGIBILITY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
A team ineligible for a postseason bowl because of NCAA and/or Big Ten sanctions is not
eligible for the Championship Game.
  a) If the ineligible team is tied for the No. 1 position, the teams in the No. 2 and No. 3
     positions are the representatives.
  b) If two or more teams are tied with an ineligible team for No. 1 or No. 2, the ineligible
     team is removed and the remaining teams revert to the tiebreak procedure.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF THE CHAMPIONSHIP GAME CANNOT BE PLAYED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The two teams with the highest winning percentage are co-champions, and the AQ representative
is determined by: head-to-head; then common conference opponents; then common opponents with
the best conference record by order of finish; then cumulative conference winning percentage of
all conference opponents (with the same unbalanced-schedule rule); then SportSource Analytics
Rating Score; then a random draw.
