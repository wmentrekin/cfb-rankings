SUN BELT CONFERENCE — FOOTBALL TIE-BREAKING PROCEDURES
Supplied by repo owner 2026-09-12. Undated in the source. THE ONLY SUPPLIED DOCUMENT FOR A
CONFERENCE THAT STILL PLAYS DIVISIONS.

FORMAT
Two seven-team divisions.
  East: App State, Coastal Carolina, Georgia Southern, Georgia State, James Madison, Marshall,
        Old Dominion
  West: Arkansas State, Louisiana, ULM, South Alabama, Southern Miss, Texas State, Troy

The Champion is decided by a game between the east and west division champions. A DIVISION
CHAMPION SHALL BE THE TEAM WITH THE HIGHEST WINNING PERCENTAGE IN ALL CONFERENCE GAMES, BOTH
DIVISIONAL AND NON-DIVISIONAL. A tie game counts as half-a-game won and half-a-game lost.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
TWO TEAM TIE FOR THE DIVISION CHAMPIONSHIP
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Head-to-head result between tied teams.
2. If still tied, the team with the HIGHEST OVERALL DIVISIONAL WINNING PERCENTAGE shall be the
   division champion.
3. If still tied, each team's winning percentage vs the team occupying the next highest position
   in the final DIVISIONAL standings, continuing down through the standings until one team
   prevails. When arriving at another group of tied teams while comparing records, use each
   team's record against the collective tied teams AS A GROUP.
4. If still tied, combined highest winning percentage against all COMMON NON-DIVISIONAL
   Conference opponents.
5. If still tied, if ONE team was ranked in the latest available CFP Selection Committee rankings
   and WINS in the final weekend of the Conference regular season, it will be declared the
   division champion. If in that same scenario the highest ranked team LOSES in the final
   weekend, a composite average of selected computer rankings (Anderson & Hester, Massey, Colley
   and Wolfe) that include all games on the final weekend will be used.
6. If there are MULTIPLE teams ranked, if the highest-ranked team WINS in the final weekend they
   will be declared the division champion. If the highest ranked team loses, the composite
   average will be used.
7. If NONE of the ranked teams win on the final weekend, the composite average will be used.
8. If NO teams are ranked going into the final weekend, the composite average will be used.
9. If still tied, the team with the highest overall (Conference and non-Conference) WINNING
   PERCENTAGE AGAINST FBS TEAMS shall be the division champion.
10. If still tied, coin toss conducted by the Commissioner.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
MULTIPLE TEAM TIE FOR THE DIVISION CHAMPIONSHIP
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
If three or more teams are tied atop a division, the following criteria are used until one team
remains and is declared the representative of the Championship game.
1. The team with the highest winning percentage IN GAMES PLAYED AMONG THE TIED TEAMS.
2. If still tied, the team with the highest overall DIVISIONAL winning percentage.
3. If still tied, each team's winning percentage vs the team occupying the next highest position
   in the final divisional standings, continuing down through the standings. Use each team's
   record against the collective tied teams as a group. If still tied, combined highest winning
   percentage against all common NON-DIVISIONAL Conference opponents.
4.-8. The same CFP-ranked / composite-computer-average cascade as the two-team steps 5-8.
9. If still tied, the team with the highest overall (Conference and non-Conference) winning
   percentage against FBS teams.
10. If still tied, the division champion will be determined by lot.
For all ties of three or more teams, the progression shall be followed until the champion amongst
the tied is determined. If one team falls out of contention for the highest seed, the remaining
teams continue down the tiebreaker.

INELIGIBLE DIVISION CHAMPION
If one or both division champions are ineligible, the divisional team with the next highest
winning percentage in all conference games shall participate. If there is a tie for the next
best overall divisional record:
1. If the ineligible team is tied for the division championship, the team it tied with shall be
   the representative.
2. If two or more teams are tied with an ineligible team, the ineligible team is removed and the
   remaining teams revert to the tie-break procedure.
3. If there is a division runner-up tie, the tied teams revert to the tiebreak procedure.

The Championship Game shall be played at the home venue of the Division Champion with highest
winning percentage in Conference games only.

HOST TIE BREAKER (a SEPARATE chain from the participant chain)
1. Head-to-head result between tied teams.
2.-5. The same CFP-ranked / composite-computer-average cascade.
6. If still tied, highest winning percentage against all common Conference opponents.
7. If still tied, highest overall (Conference and non-Conference) winning percentage against FBS
   teams.
8. If still tied, coin toss.

If at any time during this analysis any team(s) should gain an advantage over the other team(s)
tied at that position, the team(s) holding the advantage shall move forward while the other
team(s) are ELIMINATED. If it is reduced to a two-team tie at any point, the process shall revert
to the BEGINNING of the two-team procedures and shall be applied in order until broken.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (added by the implementer, not part of the source)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE MOST DEMANDING OF THE TEN. It needs three operations no other conference asks for:
- Step 2: DIVISIONAL winning percentage -- conference record restricted to divisional opponents.
  Note the division champion itself is decided on ALL conference games (divisional AND
  non-divisional), so the divisional-only record is a genuinely separate measure, not the
  primary key.
- Step 4: common opponents restricted to NON-DIVISIONAL conference opponents.
- Step 9: overall winning percentage against FBS TEAMS ONLY. Computable now that the non_fbs
  roster is plumbed through, but a different measure from both the Big 12's capped 12-game win
  count and the Mountain West's one-FCS-win-capped overall percentage. Three variants of "overall
  record, adjusted" across three conferences.
- Steps 5-8 are the CFP-ranked / final-weekend-result / computer-composite cascade shared with
  the Mountain West and the American.
Its step 3 walks the DIVISIONAL standings, not the conference standings, so frozen_order needs a
divisional scope for this conference.
The HOST chain is a separate ordered chain and is deliberately out of scope: this project renders
standings, not championship-game hosts.
