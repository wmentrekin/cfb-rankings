Supplied by repo owner 2026-09-12. Source document dated 8/1/2023 -- the ONLY supplied
Group of 5 document carrying an explicit date.

CHAMPIONSHIP GAME PARTICIPANTS & HOST DETERMINATION
The two teams that complete the Conference season with the highest winning percentages will be
the Championship Game participants. The team with the highest winning percentage outright shall
be declared the host. If there is a tie between two or more teams for participation in and/or
hosting, the following procedures shall be administered in numerical order.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIE BETWEEN TWO TEAMS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Head-to-head result between the tied teams.
   a. If the tied teams did not play each other, go to step 2.
2. Highest CFP Selection Committee Ranking (or composite of selected computer rankings).
   a. If ONE team is ranked in the latest available CFP Ranking (November 21) and WINS on the
      final weekend of the regular season, they will be declared a participant (and/or host).
      If in that same scenario the one ranked team LOSES in the final weekend, a composite
      average of selected computer rankings after all games conclude the final weekend will be
      used.
   b. If BOTH teams are ranked in the latest available CFP Ranking (November 21), the
      highest-ranked team that WINS on the final weekend will be declared a participant
      (and/or host). If NEITHER ranked team wins in the final weekend, a composite average of
      selected computer rankings after all games conclude the final weekend will be used.
   c. If NEITHER team is ranked in the latest available CFP Ranking (November 21) going into the
      final weekend, a composite average of selected computer rankings after all games conclude
      the final weekend will be used.
   For use of composite selected computer rankings, if Team A is 28.24 and Team B is 28.34,
   Team A would be declared a participant (and/or host).
3. Overall winning percentage against all opponents.
   a. A MAXIMUM OF ONE WIN against a team from the NCAA Football Championship Subdivision shall
      be included in the calculation of the winning percentage.
4. Record against the next highest-placed team in the Conference standings (based on winning
   percentage in all games played within the Conference), proceeding down the standings. When
   comparing tied teams against positions lower in the standings that are also tied, those
   lower-tied positions shall be considered a SINGLE POSITION for the purposes of comparison.
   a. If one team did not play a certain team, move to the next highest-placed team(s).
   b. If one team did not play all teams in the group of tied teams, move to the next
      highest-placed in the standings.
   c. If one team did not play the LOWEST-placed team in the standings, move to the NEXT STEP in
      the tiebreaker.
5. Winning percentage against common Conference opponents.
6. Coin toss to be conducted virtually by the Commissioner.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIE BETWEEN THREE OR MORE TEAMS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the event of a percentage tie between three or more teams, the following comparison procedure
shall be used until one or more teams gain an advantage and/or the process reverts to the
two-team tiebreaking procedures:

1. Winning percentages of the tied teams are compared in a MINI ROUND-ROBIN format. IF, WITHIN
   THE MINI ROUND-ROBIN, ANY OF THE TIED TEAMS DID NOT PLAY EACH OTHER, THE GROUP OF TEAMS SHALL
   REMAIN TIED, UNLESS ONE TEAM DEFEATED ALL OTHER TIED TEAMS.
2. Highest CFP Selection Committee Ranking (or composite average of selected computer rankings).
   The two highest-ranked tied teams will be identified using the following criteria:
   a. The tied team(s) who are ranked in the latest available CFP Ranking (November 21) and WIN
      in the final weekend.
   b. A composite average of selected computer rankings for the tied team(s) that LOSE in the
      final weekend OR were NOT ranked in the latest available CFP Ranking (November 21).
   Note: CFP Selection Committee Ranking is SUPERIOR to the composite average in (a.) and (b.).
   Example 1. If Team A is the only ranked team at 21 and wins in the final weekend, it will be
   declared a participant and would advance to a two-team tiebreaker to determine the host. The
   second team to advance will be identified using the composite of selected computer rankings.
   Example 2. If Team A is ranked 21, Team B 22, and Team C 23 and all win in the final weekend,
   Team A and B would be declared participants and advance to a two-team tiebreaker for host.
   Example 3. If Team A is 30.33, Team B 31.22, and Team C 32.11 in the composite average after
   all games conclude in the final weekend, Team A and B would be declared participants and
   advance to a two-team tiebreaker for host.
3. Overall winning percentage against all opponents.
   a. A maximum of one win against an NCAA Football Championship Subdivision team shall be
      included in the calculation.
4. Record against the next highest-placed team in the Conference standings, proceeding down the
   standings. Lower-tied positions shall be considered a single position.
   a. If one or all teams did not play a certain team, move to the next highest-placed team(s).
   b. If one or all teams did not play all teams in the group of tied teams, move to the next
      highest-place in the standings.
   c. If one or all teams did not play the lowest-placed team, move to the next step.
5. Winning percentage against common Conference opponents.
6. Drawing to be conducted virtually by the Commissioner to determine the two teams.

TIED GAMES
A football game which ends in a tie shall count as HALF-A-GAME WON AND HALF-A-GAME LOST.

ELIGIBILITY
A team ineligible under NCAA or Mountain West rules for post-season (bowl) competition shall not
compete in the Championship Game.

INABILITY TO HOST
If the host institution is unable to host, the other participant shall be declared the host.

These provisions are subject to review by the Commissioner and the Athletic Directors. 8/1/2023

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (added by the implementer, not part of the source)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
*** THIS DOCUMENT EXPLAINS THE REAL 2025 MOUNTAIN WEST FOUR-WAY TIE. ***
Multi-team step 1 does NOT rank a partially-played group by its intra-group record: if any tied
pair did not meet, "the group of teams SHALL REMAIN TIED, unless one team defeated all other
tied teams". So UNLV's 0-2 head-to-head record within the group was IRRELEVANT -- the step is
void on that shape and the tie went straight to step 2, the CFP/composite ranking. That is
exactly the outcome previously recorded as unreachable by any record-based measure, and it
confirms the prediction that only an external composite could explain it.

It also confirms the design decision to gate `sub_group_record` on
`when: round_robin_among_tied`: a bare intra-group record ranking would have placed UNLV last.

NEW OPERATIONS THIS DOCUMENT NEEDS (not in the current vocabulary):
- Step 3, overall winning PERCENTAGE with a one-FCS-win cap. The existing total_wins_capped is
  a 12-game COUNT (the Big 12's rule), not an overall percentage.
- Step 2's CFP-ranking-conditional-on-the-final-weekend-result, with a documented fall-through to
  a computer composite when the ranked team loses. Needs a rank snapshot as of a date BEFORE the
  final weekend plus each team's final-weekend result -- neither is in TiebreakContext today.
- Step 4 is record against the next highest-placed TEAM, any team, not restricted to COMMON
  opponents the way the SEC's step C is, and carries three explicit skip clauses (a/b/c) one of
  which abandons the step entirely rather than advancing within it.
