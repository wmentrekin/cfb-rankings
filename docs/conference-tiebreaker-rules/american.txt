THE AMERICAN (AMERICAN ATHLETIC CONFERENCE) — SECTION 10: CONFERENCE CHAMPION DETERMINATION
Supplied by repo owner 2026-09-12, from the conference manual (pages 27-31). Undated.

10.1 CONFERENCE COMPETITION
10.1.1 All teams will be scheduled to play eight (8) games as part of the conference schedule.
       Each team will not play five other conference teams.

10.2 TROPHY PRESENTATION
10.2.1 The American will award championship trophy to the winner of the championship game.

10.3 CHAMPIONSHIP GAME PARTICIPANT DETERMINATION
10.3.1 The two teams that complete the conference season with the highest winning percentages
       will be the participants in the Conference Championship Game.
10.3.2 The conference's tiebreaking procedures will be used to break all ties for first or
       second place.
10.3.3 The team that wins the championship game will be the American Football Champion.

10.4 CHAMPIONSHIP GAME PARTICIPANTS (UNBALANCED SCHEDULE)
10.4.1 For teams to be considered in a tie-break scenario, TEAMS MUST PLAY AT LEAST SEVEN
       CONFERENCE GAMES in years where eight games are scheduled.
10.4.2 The championship participants will be determined by the best winning percentages within
       the conference. In the event of an unbalanced schedule where there is A TIE FOR THE BEST,
       OR SECOND BEST, RECORD IN THE LOSS COLUMN, HEAD-TO-HEAD RESULTS WILL TAKE PRECEDENCE OVER
       THE WINNING PERCENTAGE as long as the required number of conference games were completed
       AND THE TEAM WITH FEWER GAMES DEFEATED ALL OTHER TIED TEAMS. If the teams remain tied, the
       tie-breaking procedures will be applied.
10.4.3 This procedure will be applied to determine the two championship game representatives and
       the championship game host.
10.4.4 This provision is subject to review by the Commissioner and the Athletic Directors from
       the institutions that are not in contention to be championship game participants.

10.5 TWO-TEAM TIE
10.5.1 If there is a two-team tie for first place, both teams will play in the championship game.
10.5.2 If there is a two-team tie for second place, the head-to-head result shall determine the
       championship game participant.
10.5.3 If there is NO head-to-head result, and ONE of the tied teams was ranked in the latest
       available CFP Selection Committee rankings and DOESN'T LOSE in the final weekend of
       regular-season conference play, it will be declared a championship game participant.
10.5.4 If there is no head-to-head result, and one of the tied teams was ranked in the latest
       available CFP rankings and LOSES in the final weekend, then a composite average of
       selected computer metrics (Connolly SP+, SportSource TR116 SOR, ESPN SOR, KPI) will be
       used.
10.5.5 If there is no head-to-head result, and BOTH tied teams are ranked in the latest available
       CFP rankings, the higher-ranked tied team that DOESN'T LOSE in the final weekend will be a
       championship game participant.
10.5.6 If there is no head-to-head result, and NEITHER of the tied ranked teams WINS in the final
       weekend, the composite average of selected computer metrics after all games conclude the
       final weekend will be used.
10.5.7 If there is no head-to-head result, and NEITHER of the tied teams is ranked in the latest
       available CFP rankings going into the final weekend, the composite average after all teams
       conclude the final weekend will be used.
10.5.8 If still tied, win percentage against all common conference opponents.
10.5.9 If still tied, the team with the highest overall winning percentage (conference and
       non-conference) EXCLUDING EXEMPT GAMES.
10.5.10 If still tied, a coin toss will be conducted by the commissioner.

10.6 MULTIPLE TEAM TIE
10.6.1 If more than two teams tie for first or second place, the tiebreaker procedures will be
       applied separately to identify the two participants in the championship game.
10.6.2 If only two teams remain tied after any step, the two-team tie procedures shall be used.
10.6.3 The conference records of the tied teams are compared in a MINI ROUND-ROBIN format. IF
       WITHIN THE MINI ROUND-ROBIN ANY OF THE TIED TEAMS DID NOT PLAY EACH OTHER, THE GROUP OF
       TEAMS SHALL REMAIN TIED UNLESS ONE TEAM DEFEATED ALL OTHER TIED TEAMS.
10.6.4 If the highest-ranked of the tied teams in the latest available CFP rankings DOESN'T LOSE
       in the final weekend, it will be declared a championship game participant.
10.6.5 If the highest-ranked of the tied teams in the latest available CFP rankings LOSES in the
       final weekend, then the composite average of selected computer metrics will be used.
10.6.6 If there are MULTIPLE tied teams ranked, the highest-ranked team or teams that WIN in the
       final weekend will be championship game participants.
10.6.7 If NONE of the ranked tied teams win in the final weekend, the composite average after all
       games conclude the final weekend will be used.
10.6.8 If NO teams are ranked going into the final weekend, the composite average after all games
       conclude the final weekend will be used.
10.6.9 If still tied, win percentage against all common conference opponents.
10.6.10 If still tied, the team with the highest overall winning percentage (conference and
        non-conference) excluding exempt games.
10.6.11 Drawing to be conducted by the Commissioner.

10.7 INELIGIBLE TEAM
10.7.1 If one or both participants are ineligible, the participant(s) shall be determined by the
       following procedure.
10.7.2 If the ineligible team is tied with another team, the team it tied with shall be the
       participant.
10.7.3 If two or more teams are tied with an ineligible team, the ineligible team is removed and
       the remaining teams revert to the tiebreaker procedure.
10.7.4 If there is a tie for second place, the tied teams shall revert to the tiebreaker.

10.8 CHAMPIONSHIP HOST DETERMINATION
10.8.1 The championship game shall be played at the home venue of the participant with the
       highest winning percentage in conference games.
10.8.2 The following tiebreaker will be used to determine the host location.

10.9 CHAMPIONSHIP HOST TIE-BREAKER (a SEPARATE chain from the participant chain)
10.9.1 Head-to-head results between tied teams.
10.9.2 If still tied, and only one team was ranked in the latest available CFP rankings and didn't
       lose in the final weekend, it will be declared the host.
10.9.3 If only one participant was ranked and loses in the final weekend, the composite average
       will be used.
10.9.4 If the higher-ranked team in the latest available CFP rankings loses in the final weekend,
       the composite average will be used.
10.9.5 If both teams are ranked, the higher-ranked team that doesn't lose in the final weekend
       will be declared the host.
10.9.6 If neither of the ranked teams wins in the final weekend, the composite average after all
       games conclude the final weekend will be used.
10.9.7 If no teams are ranked going into the final weekend, the composite average after all games
       conclude the final weekend will be used.
10.9.8 If still tied, the best win percentage against all common conference opponents.
10.9.9 If still tied, the team with the highest overall (conference and non-conference) winning
       percentage [excluding exempt games] shall be the host.
10.9.10 If still tied, a coin toss.

10.10 INABILITY TO HOST
10.10.1 If the host institution is unable to host, the other participant shall be the host.

10.11 COMMISSIONER AUTHORITY & FORFEITS
10.11.1 The Commissioner or designee will make the final decision regarding conference
        competition, including cancelling, postponing, rescheduling or terminating a contest.
10.11.2 Once a contest is to be rescheduled, members must abide. A member refusing to participate
        shall be deemed to have forfeited. If both refuse, the outcome is a double-forfeit.
10.11.3 For the purposes of conference records, standings, tie-breaking formulas and Championship
        Participation, the FORFEITING TEAM WILL BE CHARGED WITH A LOSS and the opposing team
        CREDITED WITH A WIN.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (added by the implementer, not part of the source)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
STRUCTURALLY THE MOST UNUSUAL OF THE TEN: the American's chain is dominated by the CFP-ranking
cascade. After head-to-head, FIVE consecutive clauses (10.5.3-10.5.7, and 10.6.4-10.6.8) are all
one conditional operation -- "the CFP-ranked team that doesn't lose in the final weekend, else a
computer composite" -- enumerated case by case rather than stated once. Only after all of them
does it reach a record-based measure (common opponents, 10.5.8).

Note "DOESN'T LOSE" in 10.5.3/10.5.5/10.6.4, not "wins": a tie would satisfy it, and so, read
literally, would not playing. 10.5.6 and 10.6.6/10.6.7 switch to "wins". The asymmetry appears
deliberate in the source and is preserved here rather than normalised.

TWO GATES NO OTHER CONFERENCE HAS:
- 10.4.1 is a hard ELIGIBILITY THRESHOLD: a team must have played at least 7 of 8 conference
  games to enter a tiebreak at all. Not a step -- a precondition on membership of the tied group.
- 10.4.2 is a TIE DEFINITION on the LOSS COLUMN (not win percentage, and not the ACC's
  wins-or-losses rule nor CUSA's within-one-win rule), and it INVERTS the normal priority:
  head-to-head takes precedence OVER winning percentage when the team with fewer games beat all
  other tied teams. That is the only place in any of the ten documents where a tiebreaker step
  outranks the primary standings key itself.

10.6.3 is the mini-round-robin with the "remain tied unless one team defeated all others" escape,
identical in force to the Mountain West's multi-team step 1.

"Excluding exempt games" (10.5.9/10.6.10) is the same NCAA Bylaw 17.10.5.2.1 exemption the Big 12
cites, and remains uncomputable: no available data flags an exempt game.

Forfeits (10.11.3) are assumed already reflected in the result CFBD reports; nothing here models
them separately.
