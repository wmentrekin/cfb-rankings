CONFERENCE USA — FOOTBALL REGULATIONS, SECTION 10 (CHAMPION DETERMINATION)
Supplied by repo owner 2026-09-12. Undated in the source; no era stated.

A. Conference Championship Game. The top two teams with the highest conference winning
percentage at the end of the regular season will participate in the Conference USA Football
Championship Game. If two or more teams are tied, the following six-step tiebreaker procedure
will be used to determine participants. The same criteria will be applied to determine the host
institution if the two participants have identical conference records. Member football teams
that are not bowl eligible (due to transition status, NCAA sanctions, etc.) will not have the
opportunity to compete in the conference championship football game.

B. Conference Championship Tiebreaker Procedure (Participant Selection & Hosting). In the event
of a tie for a place in the CUSA Football Championship Game, the following tiebreaking
procedures will be applied, PROVIDED ALL TEAMS PLAYED AN EQUAL NUMBER OF CONFERENCE GAMES. The
championship game participants will be determined first, followed by the selection of the host.
  1. Head-to-Head Competition: Winner of the head-to-head matchup is selected.
  2. Winning Percentage Against Common Conference Opponents: The team with the higher winning
     percentage against shared conference opponents shall be selected.
  3. Record Against the Team(s) with the Best Conference Record: If still tied, compare records
     against the common opponent(s) with the highest conference winning percentage, proceeding
     downward through the standings until the tie is broken.
  4. Average Computer Ranking: Higher average ranking across Connelly SP+, SportSource
     Analytics, ESPN SOR, and KPI Rankings.
  5. Cumulative Conference Winning Percentage of All Conference Opponents: Team whose opponents
     have the higher combined conference winning percentage is selected.
  6. Coin Toss: Conducted by the Commissioner or designee if all other steps fail.

C. Conference Championship Tiebreaker Procedure (Unequal Number of Games). In determining the
top two teams and host, the following will apply: If any team(s) has not played an equal amount
of Conference games (due to not being able to reschedule a postponed Conference game) and any
team(s) is WITHIN ONE CONFERENCE WIN from the team(s) with the highest conference winning
percentage AND HAS AN EQUAL NUMBER OF LOSSES, those teams shall be declared tied. The
championship game participants will be determined first, followed by the selection of the host
(See tiebreaker policy outlined above).

D. Multi-Team Ties. When Comparing Records Against Groups of Tied Teams: If, during the
application of the tiebreaker procedure, a set of tied teams is encountered in the standings (for
example, multiple teams tied for second place), each tied team's record shall be evaluated
COLLECTIVELY against the entire group of tied teams. If the tied teams have the same record OR
PLAYED AN UNEQUAL NUMBER OF GAMES against the teams within the tied group, immediately advance
to the team(s) with the next highest conference winning percentage. If at any point the tie is
broken in a multiple team tie, the remaining teams will begin the process again at step #1.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPLEMENTATION NOTES (added by the implementer, not part of the source)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Note the ORDER: the computer ranking is step 4, BEFORE opponents' cumulative conference win
  percentage at step 5. Every Power 4 conference has these the other way round.
- Section C is a TIE DEFINITION, not a tiebreaker step, and it differs from the ACC's: CUSA uses
  "within one conference win AND equal losses", the ACC "same wins OR same losses".
- Section D's "same record OR unequal games played -> immediately advance" is a skip condition on
  the record-against-placed-opponents step that no other conference states.
