SOUTHEASTERN CONFERENCE — OFFICIAL FOOTBALL TIEBREAKERS
(2024 onward: first seasons without divisions; announced Aug 2024)
Supplied by repo owner 2026-09-11.

In the event of a tie for a place in the Conference championship game, the following procedures
will be used in the following order:

  A. Head-to-head competition among the tied teams
  B. Record versus all common Conference opponents among the tied teams
  C. Record against highest (best) placed common Conference opponent in the Conference
     standings, and proceeding through the Conference standings among the tied teams
  D. Cumulative Conference winning percentage of all Conference opponents among the tied teams
  E. Capped relative total scoring margin (see Appendix A) per SportSource Analytics versus all
     Conference opponents among the tied teams
  F. Random draw of the tied teams

If a tiebreaker step produces standings with two teams tied for first place in the Conference,
they are both selected for the championship game. To decide the seeding of the two teams, they
progress through the Two-Team tiebreaker procedures, which determines home/away designation.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
TWO-TEAM TIE FOR SECOND PLACE -- step C sub-rules (verbatim intent)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. Record against highest (best) placed common Conference opponent, proceeding through the
   Conference standings among the tied teams
   1. If the highest place in the standings with common Conference opponents among the tied
      teams includes 2 or more opponents that are also tied:
      - The tie lower in the standings will only be broken if the Head-to-head tiebreaker
        procedure can break it.
          i.  If 2 teams are tied, they enter the beginning of the two-team procedures for 2nd.
          ii. If 3 or more are tied, they enter the beginning of the three-team (or more)
              procedures for second place.
      - If Head-to-head fails to break the tie between the teams lower in the standings, the
        records of the tied teams against these common opponents will be COMBINED.
      - If there are 3 or more opponents tied, but not all are common opponents, only the
        record against common opponents is considered.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
THREE-TEAM (OR MORE) TIE -- peel-off and restart semantics (verbatim intent)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
After the head-to-head competition among the tied teams, if a tiebreaker step produces standings
with a clear No. 1 team by itself, that team is selected for the championship game and the
remaining teams still in contention REVERT TO THE BEGINNING of the applicable tiebreaker
procedures (three tied -> the other two go to the first step of the two-team procedures for
second place; four tied -> the other three go to the first step of the three-team procedures).

If a tiebreaker step produces standings with two teams tied for No. 1, both are selected for the
championship game; they progress through the Two-Team procedures to decide seeding.

If the standings produced by any tiebreaker procedure result in a tie at the top that is
insufficient for assigning a participant, but includes one or more teams ranked BELOW the tied
teams, the tied teams at the top revert to the beginning of the tiebreaker procedures and all
other teams are ELIMINATED.

A. Head-to-head among the tied teams
   1. If there IS a complete round robin among the tied teams:
      a. One team with sole best record -> selected; two remaining -> two-team procedures for
         second; more than two remaining -> three-team procedures for second.
      b. Two teams tied for best record within the round robin -> both selected.
      c. Three or more tied for best record -> those revert to the beginning of the three-team
         procedures for first place; teams without the best record are eliminated.
   2. If there is NOT a complete round robin among the tied teams:
      a. One team beat all the other tied teams -> selected; the rest return to the start of the
         procedures for second place.
      b. One team lost to all the other tied teams -> eliminated; the rest return to the start of
         the applicable procedures, unless only two remain, in which case both are selected.
      c. If no team either beat all or lost to all -> all tied teams advance to the next step.

B. Record versus all common Conference opponents among the tied teams
   1. Sole best record -> selected; remaining teams return to the start (two -> two-team
      procedures for second; three or more -> three-team procedures for second).
   2. Two tied for best -> both selected.
   3. Three or more tied for best -> revert to the beginning of the three-team procedures for
      first place; the remaining teams are eliminated.

C. Record against highest (best) placed common Conference opponent, proceeding through the
   standings. Same sub-rules as the two-team case above. If ALL tied teams have the same record
   against the common opponent(s) with the best Conference record, proceed to the NEXT common
   Conference opponent(s) by order of finish and continue until all common opponents are
   exhausted.

D. Cumulative Conference winning percentage of all Conference opponents among the tied teams
   1. Clear first place -> selected; the rest revert to the beginning (i.e. Head-to-Head).
   2. Two-team tie for first -> both selected.
   3. More than two tied for first WITH teams below -> those tied revert to the beginning; the
      rest are eliminated.
   4. More than two tied for first with NO teams below -> advance to the next step.

E. Capped relative total scoring margin per SportSource Analytics (Appendix A)
   1. After determining the first-place team, the remaining tied teams still in contention
      revert to the start of the tiebreaker procedures.

F. Random draw of the tied teams (Athletics Directors join a videoconference; Commissioner or
   designee draws names).

Authority to interpret all tiebreaking procedures is vested only in the Commissioner or designee.

=============================================================================
APPENDIX A -- CAPPED RELATIVE SCORING MARGIN  *** FULLY SPECIFIED, COMPUTABLE ***
=============================================================================
1. At the end of the season, the score of each Conference game is evaluated based on both teams'
   average scoring offense and defense. For both teams in each game, the Conference calculates
   the relative scoring offense percentage and the relative scoring defense percentage, then
   calculates the margin (difference). This is averaged for each team across all its Conference
   games.
2. For each Conference game, the highest relative scoring OFFENSE percentage achievable is 200%,
   and the lowest relative scoring DEFENSE percentage achievable is 0%.
3. Worked example supplied by the conference:
   - Week 1: Team A defeats Team B 31-28.
   - Team B averaged 24 points scored per game for the season.
   - Team B averaged 21 points allowed per game for the season.
   - Team A's relative scoring offense: (31 / 21) * 100 = 147.6%
   - Team A's relative scoring defense: (28 / 24) * 100 = 116.7%
   - Total relative scoring margin for Team A in this game: 147.6 - 116.7 = +30.9%
   - Repeat for each Conference game for Team A, then average.
   Cap note: had Team A scored more than 42, its relative offensive percentage would exceed
   200% and would be designated 200% for that game. On defense the best possible is 0%, so no
   cap is required there.

NOTE FOR IMPLEMENTATION: despite the words "per SportSource Analytics", this step's formula is
published in full and every input (per-game scores, season scoring averages) is already in our
data. Unlike the ACC's Team Success Ranking, this is NOT a proprietary black box -- it is
computable. Only step F (random draw) is genuinely out of reach.

Appendix B of the source document contains ~25 worked examples for two-team, three-team-for-
first and three-team-for-second ties. They are conference-authored test fixtures and should be
transcribed into the test suite when step C and the peel-off semantics are implemented.
